שם ולוגו לאפליקציה

שלוש אפשרויות. כל אחת מוצגת כלוגו מלא, אייקון לטלפון ואייקון קטן.

ההמלצה שלי
FreeRise
פרי־רייז

FreeRise = rise, for free. The app's real job is seeing ahead: next month, the installments ending, retirement. A sun rising over a horizon.

Short, easy in Hebrew and English.

Zrima
זרימה

זרימה = flow. Named after the cash flow (תזרים) the whole app is built around. A wave of money moving in and out.

Friendlier, softer feel.

Matzpen
מצפן

מצפן = compass. It tells you which way your money is heading. A compass needle, half red, half white.

Note: "Matzpen" was also the name of a 1960s Israeli political group, which some people may recognize.
